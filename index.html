Memory tricks that work

Your brain is bad at storing random numbers but very good at storing pictures, places and stories. Each trick below converts digits into something your brain already likes.

1. The word code (Major System)

Think of it like Morse code: each digit gets one consonant sound. Vowels are free filler, so you can turn any pair of digits into a word you can picture. 14 is t + r, which becomes tire. 15 is t + l, which becomes towel.

Learn these ten sounds once and you can decode every word in the app:

2. The memory palace

Picture walking through your home. Like leaving sticky notes in each room, you drop one chunk's scene at each spot: front door, hallway, sofa, kitchen sink… To recite, you walk the route in your head and look at what you left there. Use the "Where in your memory palace?" box on each chunk to fix its spot.

The route keeps the order for you, which is the hardest part of remembering pi.

3. The pi poem

Count the letters in each word. They give the first 15 digits.

4. Phone-number chunks

You remember phone numbers as 3‑3‑4 groups, not ten loose digits. Every chunk in the app is split the same way, and saying it out loud gives it a rhythm, like a jingle.
